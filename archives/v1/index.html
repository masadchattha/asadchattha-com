A Little Bit About Me

Hey! My name is Asad and I'm an iOS engineer based in Lahore, Pakistan, with a soft spot for interface design. Over the last 6 years I've shipped 15+ apps to the App Store for teams in the US, UK, Ireland and Australia, across health and fitness, fintech and payments, offline mapping, encrypted messaging and VoIP, blockchain, social, on-device machine learning and productivity. I like problems where the platform itself is the hard part.

I've contributed to SwifterSwift, written a 243-page book called iOS Development using UIKit that's published on Apple Books, and I build and ship my own apps on the side.

When I'm not on the computer, I enjoy reading, long drives back to my village, and an unreasonable amount of chai.

What I Do

Design

An app is judged on how it feels long before anyone reads the code. I care about interaction detail, motion that explains rather than decorates, and interfaces that follow Apple's grain instead of fighting it. Most of my work has been on small teams where design and engineering are the same conversation.

Development

Native iOS in Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit and Objective-C, with Kotlin when a project needs Android too. I've owned the whole iOS layer more often than not, which means architecture, Core Data, networking, CI and App Store submission as much as screens. I aim for code the next engineer can read without me.

Involvement

I contribute to SwifterSwift, an open source Swift extension library, and I wrote a book on UIKit that's on Apple Books. I also build my own apps, which is the fastest way I know to find out what I don't understand yet.

Experience

6 years of iOS, and I'm still finding corners of the platform I haven't touched yet. That's most of the appeal.

Gamified screen time

Block

NFC tap to block

A physical NFC tag paired with an iOS app. Tap your phone to the tag and distracting apps lock instantly. The idea is friction: you have to walk to the tag to unblock, which is enough to break the reflex. Core NFC plus Shield Configuration for blocking that actually holds.

View on the App Store

Chapter

Replace a bad habit

A habit replacement app built around a credit system: finish something in the real world, earn screen time. Streaks, wellness logging and animated progress, with zero cloud storage so nothing leaves the device.

Bright Start

Circadian rhythm reset

Your apps stay locked until the camera confirms you've been outside in real daylight. Core ML classifies the sunlight, HealthKit tracks the effect on sleep, and a streak keeps you honest. Probably the most fun brief I've had.
